How it Works

DocuQuery uses a local RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) pipeline to read your documents. When you upload a file, it is chunked and indexed on your machine. When you ask a question, the most relevant passages are retrieved and used as context to generate a highly accurate answer.

Privacy First

Your data never leaves your device unless configured otherwise. The entire indexing and retrieval process is handled locally, ensuring that your sensitive documents remain completely private and secure.

Common Use Cases
